## Families with nothing to teach apart

These have members the data marks as genuinely interchangeable. They deliberately get a note rather than a lesson: there is no rule to learn, and inventing one would be worse than saying so.

- **Regardless (Whether A Or B)** (`regardless-a-or-b`): A うと B うと, A かれ B かれ, A であれ B であれ, A とも B とも, A にしろ B にしろ, A にせよ B にせよ, A につけ B につけ, Noun といい Noun といい, Noun といわず Noun といわず
- **Regardless Of (No Matter)** (`regardless-of`): Noun のいかんによらず, Noun によらず
